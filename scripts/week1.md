# Sabi Compound — Week 1 scripts (14 episodes)

Format: one narrator (the Dev, first person, Nigerian Pidgin) tells the whole
story and does the other characters' lines himself. Target 2.5 minutes each.
`[CLIP 2]` and `[CLIP 3]` mark where the TikTok cuts start.

Cast: **Me** (remote developer), **Mama**, **Baba Landlord**,
**Chairman** (the crypto neighbour), **Iya Bose** (provision store).

---

## EP01 — Mama Think Say I No Get Work

I dey earn dollar from my bedroom, but my mama dey pray make God give me job.

Every morning, six o'clock, she go knock my door. "You still dey sleep? Your mates don reach office!" Mama, my office na this bed. My oga dey California. E never even sleep.

But try explain am. I tell her say I be software developer. She ask me, "So na you dey repair phone?" I say no. "Computer?" No. "Then wetin you dey develop?" I say website. She look me well well, come say, "So you no get work."

[CLIP 2]

E come worse the day wey her friend, Mama Chinedu, come visit. Mama Chinedu pikin na banker. Suit, tie, ID card for neck. Every sentence, "My son for bank, my son for bank."

Then she turn face me. "And you, wetin you dey do?" Before I open mouth, my mama don answer for me. "E dey press laptop."

Press laptop! Like say I be DJ.

So I decide say I go prove am. I carry my laptop come parlour. I show them the app wey I build. People dey use am for three countries. Mama Chinedu look the screen, she nod. She say, "E fine. But where your ID card?"

I no get ID card. I get Slack.

[CLIP 3]

That night I vex. I transfer fifty thousand naira give my mama. No explanation. Just alert.

You see, na here the matter turn. Because that alert do wetin four years of explanation no fit do.

Next morning, six o'clock, nobody knock my door. Seven o'clock, nothing. I come out by ten, I see food for table. Rice. Two meat. Mama dey whisper for phone: "No disturb am, e dey work."

Compound people come dey greet me, "Engineer! Engineer!"

I think say I don win. Until Sunday, for church, pastor say make anybody wey God bless that week come out for testimony.

My mama stand up. She collect microphone. She say, "I thank God for my son. E still no get work. But the laptop don dey pay."

## EP02 — Client Wan Pay Me With Exposure

One man offer me the biggest job of my life, and the salary na zero naira.

E call me for night. Big voice. "My guy, I get project for you. E go change your life." I sit up. I say, sir, wetin be the project? E say e wan build app. Like Uber. But for okada. And for keke. And for canoe. All inside one app.

I calculate am for head. Six months work. I tell am the price.

Line just quiet. I think say network cut. Then e laugh. "My brother, no be money matter be this. Na exposure."

[CLIP 2]

Exposure. I ask am, sir, this exposure, landlord dey collect am? E say, "You no dey see vision. When this app blow, everybody go know your name."

I say okay sir, make I ask you one thing. The driver wey go carry you come our meeting, you go pay am with exposure? E say that one different.

The mechanic wey repair your car? Different. The woman wey sell you this recharge card wey you take dey call me? "Ah, that one na business."

So na only me be charity.

But the man no give up. E come my compound. Physically. E meet my mama. E tell her say her son dey refuse opportunity. My own mama turn face me: "Why you dey stubborn? The man say you go popular!"

[CLIP 3]

So I say okay. I go do am. But I get one condition. I go build the app, and I go put my own name for the front page. Big. Since na exposure we dey do.

E happy. E sign. E no read.

You see, the thing wey e no know be say, when you no pay for something, you no own am.

Three months later, app ready. E launch am with canopy, with DJ, with small chops. E press the app for stage make everybody see.

First screen show: "This app belong to the developer. Owner never pay."

The whole crowd read am. The DJ stop music.

E turn look me. I just wave.

I say, "Sir. Exposure."

## EP03 — NEPA Take Light As I Dey Deploy

The most dangerous button for my life na the one wey dey write "Deploy," and NEPA sabi exactly when I wan press am.

Friday. Four o'clock. My oga for abroad say the update must go live today. Na one small change. Five minutes work. I check my laptop battery: twelve percent. No wahala, light dey.

I put hand for button. I press am.

Na that same second, everywhere go dark. Fan dey slow down, whoo, whoo, whoo. For the whole street you go hear am: "NEPA!"

[CLIP 2]

Now my deploy don reach forty percent. If e stop for middle, the whole website go spoil. Ten thousand customers. My battery: nine percent.

I run go meet generator. I draw am. E cough. I draw am again. E cough twice. I open the tank. Dry. Like harmattan.

I run go Iya Bose shop. "Iya Bose, abeg, fuel!" She say fuel don finish, but she get candle. Candle! Wetin I wan do, light my router?

Battery: six percent.

I remember Chairman, my neighbour. E get inverter. I knock. E open door small, e say, "I fit charge your laptop. Five hundred naira per percent."

Per percent! This one no be neighbour, na Shylock.

[CLIP 3]

I pay am. Wetin I go do? I siddon for im corridor, laptop for my leg, dey watch the bar. Sixty percent. Seventy. Battery: three percent. My heart dey do like drum.

Ninety. Ninety-five. Battery: one percent.

Hundred. "Deploy successful."

I shout. Chairman shout. Even Iya Bose wey no know wetin dey happen, she shout join.

And as I dey shout, light come back. Fan start. Fridge hum. Whole street: "Up NEPA!"

I enter house, I breathe. Phone ring. Na my oga.

I say, "Sir, e don go live. E no easy, but I do am."

E say, "Great work. But we see small typo. Abeg fix am, deploy again now now."

I look the bulb.

The bulb blink.

## EP04 — Landlord See My Laptop

My house rent increase by one hundred thousand naira because my landlord see wetin dey my hand.

For three years, I dey hide am. Whenever Baba Landlord dey come, I go close laptop, carry exercise book, dey look like student. Because for this compound, Baba Landlord no dey charge rent by room. E dey charge by face.

If you buy new curtain, rent go up. You paint your door, rent go up. One woman buy standing fan, e tell her say, "Madam, you don dey comfortable."

[CLIP 2]

So that day, I dey video call with my client. Headphone for ear. I no hear the gate. I no hear the slippers.

I just see shadow for my window.

Baba Landlord stand there. E dey look my screen. Oyinbo man dey talk for inside. E look the oyinbo. E look me. E look the oyinbo again.

E no talk anything. E just smile, waka go.

That smile cost me money.

Evening, I see letter under my door. "Dear tenant. Due to your international connections, your rent don review."

International connections! Baba, na Zoom!

I go meet am. I explain. I say the man na my client, e dey pay small small. Baba Landlord say, "If white man dey inside your room, you be expatriate. Expatriate rent different."

[CLIP 3]

I vex. I say fine. If na so, I go pack. I begin find new house.

But you see, Baba Landlord get one problem wey e no tell anybody. E get twelve tenants and e no sabi who don pay. Everything dey inside one old notebook, and rain don beat the notebook.

So one evening e knock my door. Quiet. No shouting. E say, "This your laptop. E fit remember who dey owe me?"

I build am small app. One evening work. Every tenant, every payment, every date.

E happy sotay e dance. E say, "From today, your rent don reduce!"

I smile. I open the app make I confirm.

Na there I see the new line e don add by himself:

"Laptop boy. App maintenance fee: one hundred thousand."

## EP05 — Chairman Sure Deal

My neighbour don become millionaire four times, and e still dey owe me two thousand naira.

Everybody call am Chairman. Nobody know chairman of wetin. Every week e get new business. Last month na coin. The month before, na forex. Before that one, e dey sell water wey e say fit charge phone.

Monday morning e knock my door. E wear suit. For this heat. E say, "My guy. This one sure. Hundred percent."

I say Chairman, the last one too sure. E say, "That one na ninety-nine percent. This one na hundred."

[CLIP 2]

The deal be like this. You bring ten thousand. After two weeks you collect twenty. I ask am, where the money dey come from? E say, "From the system."

Which system? "The system wey dey inside."

I be developer. I know system. This one no be system, na prayer.

But the compound no hear word. Iya Bose put her shop money. Baba Landlord put two months rent. Even my own mama bring her esusu contribution come. She say, "Chairman, double am for me."

Na only me refuse. Everybody dey look me like say I be enemy of progress. My mama say, "You wey go school, you no wan make your family rich?"

[CLIP 3]

Two weeks reach. The whole compound gather for Chairman door. E no open. Phone switch off. Iya Bose don already carry pestle.

You see, na that time I do wetin I sabi do. I trace the website. Two minutes. The so-called system na one free page, and the owner na one small boy for cyber cafe for the next street.

We march go there. Chairman dey there. E dey shout for the boy too. E put im own money join! The man wey bring the scam, na the scam chop am first.

Na so we collect the boy. E return everybody money. Complete.

Evening, compound quiet. Everybody dey thank me. Chairman come knock my door. Head down. I think say e wan apologise.

E say, "My guy. You sabi trace website. You know how much people go pay for that one? I get idea. Hundred percent."

## EP06 — Dollar Rate Change Overnight

I sleep as rich man, I wake up as poor man, and I no spend one kobo for night.

Na so the matter be. My client pay me for dollar. Friday night, I check the rate. E sweet. I calculate am, I see say this month, I go pay rent, buy generator, still chop shawarma.

I say make I change the money tomorrow morning. Wetin fit happen for one night?

For this country? Everything.

[CLIP 2]

Saturday, I wake up, I open phone. The rate don fall. No be small fall. Na the kind fall wey person dey hold chest.

My generator don become rechargeable lamp. My shawarma don become gala.

I call the aboki wey dey change money. I say, "Abeg, wetin happen?" E say, "Oga, na market." I say which market open for midnight? E say, "Dollar no dey sleep."

So I decide say I go wait. E go rise back. I siddon dey refresh. Every five minutes. Refresh. E fall again. Refresh. E fall again.

My mama pass, she see my face. She say, "Who die?" I say, "Dollar." She say, "E be your family?"

By afternoon, Chairman don hear. E come give me advice. "Hold am. No sell. Rate go climb by Monday. Trust me."

That one wey Chairman talk, I know say I must sell immediately.

[CLIP 3]

But I no sell. I stubborn. Sunday, e fall again. I no fit chop. Rice dey my front, I dey see exchange rate inside the stew.

Monday morning, I give up. I change everything. Small money, but I collect am. I say at least the matter don end.

You see, the thing about this rate be say, e dey watch you. Personally.

Ten minutes after I change am, my phone do "ping." Rate don rise. Pass Friday own. Pass any rate wey I don ever see for my life.

Chairman run enter my room dey jump. "I tell you! I tell you make you hold!"

For the first time for im whole life, Chairman correct.

And na my money e take correct.

## EP07 — Iya Bose Wan App

One woman wey no sabi read English give me the hardest software job wey I don ever collect.

Iya Bose get provision store for our street front. She sabi everybody. Who dey owe, who dey lie, who buy indomie on credit since January. Everything dey for her head. No book. No biro.

One day she call me. "Engineer! This your laptop work. Do app for me."

I say Iya Bose, which kind app? She say, "The one wey dey catch debtor."

[CLIP 2]

I think say e go easy. List of names, amount, finish. I build am one night. I carry am go show her.

She look am. She hiss. "Wetin be this? Where Mama Tunde?" I say she dey there, number four. She say, "No. Mama Tunde no be number. Mama Tunde na the one wey dey always say her husband never send money. Put that one."

So I add notes.

She say, "And Baba Landlord. When e buy something, e dey say make I 'put am down'. Put am down, put am down, since last year. Your app fit shout for am?"

Shout? She want make the phone dey call debtor name loud when dem dey pass.

I say Iya Bose, app no dey do that kind thing. She say, "Then wetin you go school go learn?"

[CLIP 3]

Three weeks. Fourteen versions. This woman reject pass any oyinbo client wey I don ever get. No contract, no email. Just "E no sweet me. Do am again."

Finally I finish. The app sabi everybody. E dey remind her. E even dey talk Yoruba.

She press am. She smile. First time. She say, "Engineer, you try."

You see, na that time I make my mistake. I ask her, "So Iya Bose, about my payment…"

She no answer. She just pick her phone. She open the app. My own app. She scroll, scroll, come turn the screen face me.

My name dey there. Number one.

"Engineer. Indomie, four. Egg, six. Malt, two. Since March."

She say, "We don balance."

## EP08 — Generator Fuel Or Data

I get five thousand naira, and I must choose between light and internet. I no fit get the two.

Na Thursday. My salary never enter. For my account, five thousand. For my generator, fuel don finish. For my phone, data remain two hundred MB. And by eight o'clock tonight, I get meeting with client wey fit give me six months contract.

Simple maths. Fuel na five thousand. Data na five thousand.

If I buy fuel, I go get light, but no internet. I go just siddon for bright room dey look wall.

If I buy data, I go get internet, but my laptop go die for middle of meeting.

[CLIP 2]

I try be smart. I go meet Chairman. I say, "You get inverter. Make I charge." E say im inverter don spoil, but e fit sell me power bank. I look am. The power bank na empty case. Sand dey inside.

I go meet Iya Bose. "Abeg, borrow me data." She say, "Na sugar? How person dey borrow data?"

I try the neighbour Wi-Fi. Password don change. Dem name the network, "Pay your own."

Time: seven o'clock. One hour remain.

So I buy data. I say I go manage battery. I reduce brightness sotay I no fit see my own screen. I off Bluetooth. I off everything. I dey breathe small small make laptop no hear.

[CLIP 3]

Eight o'clock. Meeting start. Client face show. Battery, fifteen percent.

E dey talk long. "Tell me about yourself." Oga, no time! Ten percent. I dey answer fast fast like auctioneer.

Seven percent. E say, "One last question." Five percent. Three.

You see, as I wan answer, something happen wey I no plan for. Light come. NEPA! For the first time since Monday! I plug charger. I smile like person wey win election.

The client smile too. E say, "You are hired. One small thing. We like your energy, but your video too dark. Next time, abeg, on your light."

I say yes sir.

I end the call. I stand up make I on the bulb.

Na that time NEPA take am back.

## EP09 — Danfo Hotspot

I attend the most important meeting of my career inside a yellow bus, and the conductor nearly collect my job.

My client change the meeting time. No warning. Nine o'clock sharp. And nine o'clock, I dey inside danfo for Third Mainland Bridge, traffic no dey move.

No wahala. I get laptop, I get hotspot. I open am for my leg. Woman for my left carry live chicken. Man for my right dey sleep for my shoulder.

I join the call. I mute. I smile.

[CLIP 2]

First problem. Conductor. This man voice no get volume button. "Oshodi! Oshodi! Enter with your change o!" Every time I unmute, na im e go shout.

My client ask, "Where are you?" I say, "Co-working space." Chicken answer for me.

Second problem. The whole bus don see laptop. Everybody turn. One boy for back say, "Bros, abeg, your hotspot. Na just WhatsApp." I say no. E say, "God go bless you." I say amen, but no.

Next thing, my data dey run like tap. I check am. Six devices connect. Six! The woman with chicken dey watch film. Even the driver dey download something.

My password na my surname, and my surname dey my bag.

[CLIP 3]

Now video dey hang. My client face freeze with mouth open. E say, "Your network is unstable." I say, "Na the… co-working space, sir. E full today."

Conductor tap me. "Oga. Your money." I give am one thousand. E say e no get change. I say, "Abeg, I dey meeting." E say, "Me too I dey work."

You see, the thing wey save me no be wetin I expect.

Client say, "Before we continue, we need an idea for the transport app. Something real."

I look the bus. The conductor wey no get change. The driver wey no know which road free. Six people wey dey find network.

I just describe wetin dey my front. Everything.

Client quiet. Then e say, "This is brilliant. Where did you get this research?"

Conductor shout, "Oshodi!"

I say, "Field work, sir."

## EP10 — Borrow Me Money Season

The day my salary enter, eleven people wey I never see for six months remember my birthday. My birthday na for March. This one na October.

I no tell anybody. I swear. The alert just enter, "ping", and before I finish read am, my phone begin ring.

First na my cousin. "Bros! Long time! I just say make I check on you." Check on me. This guy never check on me since secondary school.

Second na my old classmate. Third na one number wey I no save. E say, "Guess who?" I no wan guess. Guessing dey cost money.

[CLIP 2]

How dem take know? I begin investigate. I be developer, I dey debug. Who see the alert?

Then I remember. I buy bread for Iya Bose shop this morning. I pay with transfer. She see my balance for the screen.

Iya Bose na the compound network. Faster pass fibre.

By afternoon, queue don form for my door. Real queue. Chairman need "small capital." Baba Landlord say roof wan spoil, tenants must contribute. My aunty call say the goat for village sick. Goat! I be vet?

Everybody get story. Everybody story end the same way: "Just till month end."

Which month end? Dem no talk the year.

[CLIP 3]

So I form plan. I go broke before dem reach me. I pay my rent six months. I buy fuel. I buy foodstuff full house. I leave only two thousand for the account, then I screenshot am.

Anybody wey ask, I show am the screenshot. "See. Me sef I dey find who go borrow me."

E work! Queue scatter. Phone quiet. I siddon for my house, full of food, dey laugh.

You see, wetin I forget be say Iya Bose no dey sleep on information.

Evening, knock for door. I open. The whole compound stand there. With plates.

Iya Bose dey front. She say, "Engineer. We hear say you no get money again."

I say yes o, nothing dey.

She say, "No wahala. We no come for money. We come for the rice."

## EP11 — Fake Alert

One man pay me two hundred thousand naira, my phone confirm am, my bank say dem no see anything.

I sell my old laptop. I post am online. One guy call, e say e go come that same day. E come with fine car, fine perfume, fine English. E no even price am. That one suppose warn me. Nigerian wey no price? Something dey.

E check the laptop two minutes. E say, "I go transfer now." E press im phone. My phone do "ping."

Credit alert. Two hundred thousand. E shake my hand, carry laptop, enter car.

[CLIP 2]

As e dey reverse, something dey do me for body. I look the alert again. The message say "Dear Costumer."

Costumer. With O.

My bank fit vex me, but dem sabi spell.

I open my banking app. Balance: the same four thousand wey dey there since morning.

I shout, "Hold am!" The car don reach gate.

You see this compound? We dey fight each other every day. But if outsider touch one person, everybody turn soldier.

Iya Bose block the gate with her freezer. Baba Landlord lock the padlock, put the key for im pocket. Chairman lie down for front of the car. Nobody ask am to.

[CLIP 3]

The guy come down. E still dey form. "Check your account, network dey slow." I say, okay, make we wait for the network. E say e dey rush. Baba Landlord say, "Nobody dey rush for my compound. Even rent dey wait."

So we siddon. One hour. Iya Bose bring bench. She begin sell am pure water. E buy two.

Finally the guy tire. E say okay, e go do real transfer. E press phone. My phone ping. This time, I check the app first. E enter. Complete.

We open gate. Everybody clap. I thank them. I tell them say I go buy drinks for the whole compound.

You see, the matter suppose end there. But as I enter my room, I remember one small thing.

That laptop wey I sell am.

I never log out.

By night, I open my new laptop, I see im screen. I see im chats. I see the next person wey e wan send "costumer" alert.

I type for am: "Dear Costumer. Compound dey watch you."

## EP12 — Cousin Wan Free Website

My cousin ask me for small favour. The small favour take four months, and e still dey vex for me.

E call me Sunday evening. Sweet voice. "Bros, you know say you be the only tech person for this family." When person start like that, hold your wallet.

E say e wan start business. E dey sell shoe. E need website. "Simple one. Just small thing. Like Amazon."

Like Amazon. Small thing.

I say okay, how much you budget? E laugh. "Budget ke? We be family."

[CLIP 2]

I for refuse. But e don already call my mama. And my mama call me. "Your own blood. You go charge am? Na so dem train you?"

So I start. Free. Week one, I show am design. E say the blue no blue enough. I change am. E say now e too blue.

Week three, e say make the logo big. Week four, bigger. By week six, the logo don cover the shoe. E say, "Yes! Now you dey get am."

E dey call me midnight. "Bros, I get idea. Make the website dey play music when person open am." I say no. E report me to my mama again.

Then e send me voice note, four minutes, to explain say the button suppose "feel more expensive."

[CLIP 3]

Month four, website ready. E fine. Honestly, na one of my best work. I tell am say remain just the hosting, twenty thousand per year, make e pay make the site go live.

E quiet. Then e say, "So after everything, you still wan collect money from me?"

Twenty thousand! No be even for me, na for the server!

E vex. E tell the whole family say I do am strong thing. For family WhatsApp group, my aunty type, "Money don change am." Uncle add, "Na so dem dey start."

You see, I no fight. I just do one thing. I pay the twenty thousand myself. Site go live. I send the link to the family group.

Everybody open am. Everybody praise am. My cousin begin collect orders that same night.

Next morning e call me. Sweet voice again.

"Bros. Customers dey ask for app."

## EP13 — Sunday Rice And Client Call

For my house, nothing on this earth important reach Sunday rice. Not even the client wey dey pay for the rice.

Sunday, two o'clock. Mama don dey cook since morning. Jollof. The one wey the bottom dey burn small, the correct one. The smell don enter my room, don enter my brain.

Na that time my phone ring. Client. Emergency. "Our site is down. We need you now."

I look the phone. I look the kitchen.

Mama shout, "Food don ready! If you no come now, I no go call you twice!"

[CLIP 2]

For this house, that statement na law. If you miss the first call, meat don finish. My small brother dey there. That boy dey chop like say dem dey pursue am.

So I carry the laptop come dining table. I say I go do the two. One hand for keyboard, one hand for spoon.

Mama look me. "You bring work come my table?" I say Mama, na emergency. She say, "Na so your papa dey do. See as e be."

My papa dey sit down there. E just dey chew.

Client dey video call. I off my camera. I dey type with left hand. Server error everywhere. My brother don carry my meat. I see am. I no fit talk, I dey for call.

[CLIP 3]

Then client say, "Please turn on your camera, we need to see your screen." I on am by mistake. The whole dining table show. Mama, Papa, my brother with two meat for mouth, and one big pot of jollof.

Silence.

Client say, "Is that… jollof rice?"

Before I answer, Mama collect the laptop. She face the camera. "Good afternoon. You dey disturb my son for Sunday. You don chop?"

Client say no, e never chop, na since morning e dey work.

You see, na here the meeting change. My mama begin lecture am. Twenty minutes. About rest. About family. About how money no be everything.

The client dey nod. E even dey write am down.

Site come back by itself. E say, "Your mother is very wise. Give her my regards."

Monday, I open my email. Na from the client.

"Please, can your mother join our next team meeting?"

## EP14 — Bank App Down For Payday

My salary enter today. I fit see am. I just no fit touch am.

Thirtieth of the month. Six a.m. Alert enter. I smile for my sleep. I get plans. Rent. Fuel. And one particular suya wey I don dey eye for two weeks.

I open the bank app make I transfer. The thing dey spin. Dey spin. Then e write: "Service temporarily unavailable."

Temporarily. For bank, that word no get meaning.

[CLIP 2]

I try again. "Something went wrong." I know say something go wrong, tell me wetin!

I try the USSD code. E say, "Dear customer, please try again later." I call customer care. Music. One woman voice: "Your call is important to us." If e important, pick am!

Forty minutes. Same song. I don sabi the lyrics.

Meanwhile, Baba Landlord don dey knock. "Today na thirtieth." I say Baba, money dey, na the app. E say, "Every month na the app."

I go ATM. The queue long reach next street. Person for my front say e don dey there since yesterday. E carry mat.

I reach front after two hours. Machine swallow my card. E no give money. E no give card. E just write, "Thank you for banking with us."

[CLIP 3]

I waka go the bank itself. Security say network down. I say, so wetin una dey do inside? E say, "We dey wait for network."

I sit down for the bank floor. I be developer. I dey build this kind app for oyinbo people, and their own no dey fall. I vex sotay I open my laptop right there. I check their app, just to see wetin dey worry am.

You see, I no suppose find anything. But the error show me the name of the company wey build am.

I read am twice.

Na my company. Na my team. Na the update wey I deploy for Friday. With the typo.

My phone ring. My oga. "The bank app is down. Who pushed on Friday?"

I look the queue. Two hundred people. One mat.

I say, "Sir. Network bad. I no hear you."

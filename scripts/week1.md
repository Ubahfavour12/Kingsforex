# Sabi Compound — Week 1: Meet the Compound

Seven episodes, about 2.5 minutes each (roughly 330–360 spoken words).
`[CLIP 1/2/3]` marks where each TikTok cut starts. Stage directions are in *italics*.

Cast: **SABI** (the dev, deadpan), **MAMA**, **LANDLORD**, **CRYPTO GUY** (Chidi), **IYA SHOP**.

---

## Episode 1 — Mama Think Say I No Get Work

**[CLIP 1]**
**SABI (narration):** I dey earn dollar from my bedroom, but my mama believe say I be jobless.

*Morning. Sabi at his desk in boxers and a shirt, laptop open. Mama enters with a broom.*

**MAMA:** Sabi! Na 10 o'clock. Your mates don reach office. You still dey press laptop.

**SABI:** Mama, I dey work.

**MAMA:** Work? Which work person dey do for bed? Who be your oga? Where the office dey?

**SABI:** My oga dey Canada. The office dey inside the laptop.

**MAMA:** Inside laptop. *(to the ceiling)* God, see my life. I train this boy for university, him office dey inside laptop.

**[CLIP 2]**
*Compound yard. Mama is telling Iya Shop.*

**MAMA:** Iya Shop, abeg, if you hear of any vacancy, tell me. Even na security.

**IYA SHOP:** Ah! Sabi wey dey press computer day and night?

**MAMA:** Na the pressing be the problem! Pressing no be salary.

*Sabi walks out on a call, headset on.*

**SABI:** Yes, I'll push the fix before your stand-up. No problem.

**MAMA:** You see? Him dey talk to himself now. E don reach that level.

**IYA SHOP:** Mama Sabi, make we carry am go church.

**[CLIP 3]**
*Night. Sabi's phone buzzes. He shows Mama the screen.*

**SABI:** Mama. Look.

**MAMA:** Wetin be this? ... Wait. Na naira be this or phone number?

**SABI:** Na salary. For this month.

*Long silence. Mama sits down slowly.*

**MAMA:** From the laptop?

**SABI:** From the laptop.

**MAMA:** *(stands, adjusts wrapper)* Iya Shop! IYA SHOP! Come hear! My pikin na international staff! Him office dey abroad, na only him body dey Nigeria!

**SABI (narration):** You see, na so my mama finally understand remote work.

*Mama pokes her head back in.*

**MAMA:** Sabi. Since the office dey inside the laptop... buy second laptop. Make you get two office.

## Episode 2 — NEPA Take Light As I Dey Deploy

**[CLIP 1]**
**SABI (narration):** The most dangerous thing for software engineering no be hacker. Na NEPA.

*Night. Sabi at his desk. Progress bar on screen: "Deploying… 87%".*

**SABI:** 87 percent. Client dey wait. Just three more minutes. Nobody breathe.

*Everything goes black. Silence. One mosquito.*

**SABI:** ...No.

**COMPOUND (off-screen, many voices):** NEPAAAA!

**[CLIP 2]**
*Sabi checks the laptop: "Battery 4%". Checks the phone: "Data balance: 12MB".*

**SABI:** Four percent battery. Twelve MB. God, na test be this?

*He runs outside to the generator. Pulls the rope. Nothing. Pulls again. Nothing.*

**LANDLORD:** *(from his window)* That gen no get fuel. And na you use am last.

**SABI:** Landlord, abeg, I get emergency. If this thing no finish, I go lose client.

**LANDLORD:** Fuel na 1,200 per litre. Emergency na extra 500.

**SABI:** You dey charge me for emergency?

**LANDLORD:** Na business. You sef dey charge your client.

**[CLIP 3]**
*Sabi sprints to Iya Shop, who is sitting by a rechargeable lamp and a noisy small generator.*

**SABI:** Iya Shop! Abeg, make I charge. Two minutes!

**IYA SHOP:** Charging na 200 naira.

**SABI:** Take 500. Keep change.

*He plugs in, kneels on the floor between cartons of noodles, opens the laptop. "Deploying… 99%… Done."*

**SABI:** *(whisper)* E don enter.

*His phone rings. Client.*

**CLIENT (voice):** Great job! So smooth. You must have an amazing setup over there.

*Wide shot: Sabi on the floor of the shop, one bulb, generator smoke, a goat watching.*

**SABI:** Yes. World-class infrastructure.

*The lights in the whole street come back on.*

**COMPOUND:** UP NEPAAA!

**SABI (narration):** You see, for this country, your real senior engineer na the person wey get fuel.

## Episode 3 — Landlord Wan Collect Tech Rent

**[CLIP 1]**
**SABI (narration):** My rent increase because my landlord see me dey use laptop.

*Compound yard. Landlord is inspecting the wall with his hands behind his back. Sabi is typing outside, enjoying the breeze.*

**LANDLORD:** Sabi. This your computer. Na the big type?

**SABI:** Na normal laptop, sir.

**LANDLORD:** Normal. And e dey bring money?

**SABI:** Small small.

**LANDLORD:** Hmm. Small small. *(writes something in a tiny notebook)*

**[CLIP 2]**
*Next morning. A paper is pasted on Sabi's door: "NOTICE: TECH RENT."*

**SABI:** Landlord! Wetin be tech rent?

**LANDLORD:** You dey use my house do international business. My roof dey cover your dollar. So rent don change.

**SABI:** Na the same room! Same leaking roof!

**LANDLORD:** Ehen. The roof wey dey leak dey leak on top dollar now. E don become premium leak.

**SABI:** Crypto Guy sef dey do business for him room!

*Cut to Crypto Guy's window: he is staring at a red chart, crying quietly.*

**LANDLORD:** That one dey lose money. I dey pity am. Him rent reduce.

**[CLIP 3]**
*Sabi thinks. Then smiles.*

**SABI:** Okay sir. No wahala. But if na tech house, then you be tech landlord. I go build website for the compound.

**LANDLORD:** Website?

**SABI:** Every tenant go fit report problem. Leaking roof. Broken tap. No light. And the whole world go see am. With your name.

*Landlord imagines it: a webpage, "LANDLORD RATING: 1 STAR ★".*

**LANDLORD:** ...The whole world?

**SABI:** Including your village people.

*Landlord quietly peels the notice off the door.*

**LANDLORD:** You know, Sabi, you be like son to me. Old rent dey okay.

**SABI (narration):** You see, na so I learn say for negotiation, data na power.

*Landlord turns back.*

**LANDLORD:** But that website... e fit collect rent for me automatic?

## Episode 4 — Crypto Guy Get Sure Deal

**[CLIP 1]**
**SABI (narration):** My neighbour don become millionaire eleven times. Him still dey borrow my charger.

*Crypto Guy bursts into Sabi's room without knocking.*

**CRYPTO GUY:** Sabi! Drop everything. This one na the one.

**SABI:** Chidi, you talk that one last week.

**CRYPTO GUY:** Last week na practice. This coin, Sabi... e go do 100x. Before Friday.

**SABI:** Wetin be the name?

**CRYPTO GUY:** GoatCoin.

**SABI:** ...Goat.

**CRYPTO GUY:** Goat! Greatest Of All Time! The name alone na signal!

**[CLIP 2]**
*Crypto Guy draws a chart in the air with his finger.*

**CRYPTO GUY:** Look. E dey here. E go reach here. Simple mathematics.

**SABI:** Who build am?

**CRYPTO GUY:** Anonymous developer. Very serious person.

**SABI:** How you take know say him serious?

**CRYPTO GUY:** Him profile picture na lion.

**SABI:** Chidi. I be developer. I fit create coin for thirty minutes and call am JollofCoin. E no mean say e get value.

**CRYPTO GUY:** *(long pause)* ...JollofCoin. Sabi. You be genius. Make we launch am.

**SABI:** That no be the point!

**[CLIP 3]**
*Friday. Crypto Guy at Sabi's door, very calm, holding a nylon bag.*

**SABI:** Friday don reach. How GoatCoin?

**CRYPTO GUY:** The goat don travel.

**SABI:** Travel.

**CRYPTO GUY:** The developer comot with everybody money. Lion profile picture, na so him use am chop us like lion.

**SABI:** How much you lose?

**CRYPTO GUY:** I no lose. I gain experience.

**SABI:** How much the experience cost?

**CRYPTO GUY:** My rent.

*Silence. Crypto Guy puts the nylon bag on Sabi's bed.*

**CRYPTO GUY:** So I go dey stay with you small. Just till the market recover.

**SABI (narration):** You see, for crypto, dem say make you no invest wetin you no fit lose. Nobody warn the neighbour.

**CRYPTO GUY:** *(already lying down)* Sabi. About that JollofCoin...

## Episode 5 — Iya Shop Wan App

**[CLIP 1]**
**SABI (narration):** A woman wey dey sell bread wan make I build Facebook for her. Budget: two thousand naira.

*Iya Shop's store. Sabi is buying bread.*

**IYA SHOP:** Sabi. Dem say you dey build app.

**SABI:** Yes ma.

**IYA SHOP:** Good. Build one for me. I want make people dey order from house. Make e get video, make e get chat, make e show me who dey owe me.

**SABI:** Okay... that na big work. Wetin be your budget?

**IYA SHOP:** Two thousand. And I go add one Gala.

**[CLIP 2]**
**SABI:** Iya Shop, app like that fit cost millions.

**IYA SHOP:** Millions?! Na button you dey press! I dey see you. Tap tap tap. You never even sweat.

**SABI:** Na my brain dey sweat.

**IYA SHOP:** Okay. Three thousand. And two Gala. Final.

**SABI:** Ma, wetin be the real problem wey you wan solve?

**IYA SHOP:** *(sighs, brings out a huge, torn exercise book)* Na this. Debtors. Everybody for this compound dey owe me. Landlord: sardine, since March. Chidi: indomie, eleven packs. Your mama: Maggi.

**SABI:** My mama?

**IYA SHOP:** Page four.

**[CLIP 3]**
*Sabi takes her small phone, taps for a moment, hands it back.*

**SABI:** Oya. No need app. I don set am for your WhatsApp. Every Friday, e go send message give everybody wey dey owe you. Automatic.

**IYA SHOP:** Just like that? No millions?

**SABI:** Just like that.

*Friday. Phones buzz all over the compound. Landlord reads his and drops his chewing stick. Crypto Guy hides behind a drum. Mama clutches her chest.*

**MAMA:** Who teach this woman technology?!

*People queue at the shop with money. Iya Shop is counting cash, beaming.*

**IYA SHOP:** Sabi! You be correct pikin. Take your payment.

*She hands him two Gala. And a small paper.*

**SABI:** Wetin be this?

**IYA SHOP:** Your own bill. Bread. Since January.

**SABI (narration):** You see, the best technology na the one wey solve the real problem. Even when the problem na you.

## Episode 6 — Danfo Wi-Fi Hotspot

**[CLIP 1]**
**SABI (narration):** I do one meeting with my oga for Canada, inside Danfo, for Third Mainland Bridge. Nobody for that meeting know.

*Danfo interior, packed. Sabi squeezed between a big man and a woman with a live chicken. Phone alarm: "Client call in 2 mins."*

**SABI:** No no no. Traffic no dey move.

**CONDUCTOR:** Oshodi! Oshodi! Enter with your change o!

**SABI:** *(to everyone)* Abeg. I get important call. Five minutes. Make everybody just... calm down.

**BIG MAN:** Na you get the bus?

**[CLIP 2]**
*Sabi joins the call, phone held close, a blurred office background filter on.*

**CLIENT (voice):** Hi! Can you hear me? Where are you today?

**SABI:** *(posh voice)* In transit. Between offices.

*The chicken squawks loudly.*

**CLIENT (voice):** What was that?

**SABI:** Notification sound.

**CONDUCTOR:** *(leaning into frame)* Oga, your money!

**SABI:** *(whisper)* I go pay! *(posh)* Sorry, that was... accounts department.

**CLIENT (voice):** Wow, your finance team is aggressive.

**SABI:** You have no idea.

**[CLIP 3]**
*The network bar drops to zero. "Reconnecting…"*

**SABI:** No network! Abeg, who get data? I go pay!

*The whole bus looks at each other. The woman with the chicken slowly raises her phone.*

**CHICKEN WOMAN:** Hotspot. 500 naira.

**SABI:** Deal!

*He reconnects. The bus goes quiet. Everyone is now listening to the meeting.*

**CLIENT (voice):** So can you deliver by Monday?

*Sabi hesitates. The Big Man nods at him firmly. The conductor gives a thumbs up. The chicken woman mouths "talk yes".*

**SABI:** Yes. Monday.

**CLIENT (voice):** Fantastic. Great meeting.

*Call ends. The whole Danfo erupts in cheering.*

**BIG MAN:** Our pikin don close deal!

**SABI (narration):** You see, na so I discover say Lagos na the biggest co-working space for the world.

**CONDUCTOR:** Oga. Since na office you turn my bus... add office rent join your fare.

## Episode 7 — Sunday Rice and Client Call

**[CLIP 1]**
**SABI (narration):** For Nigeria, one thing pass any deadline. Sunday rice.

*Sunday afternoon. The whole compound smells of stew: wavy aroma lines drift into Sabi's room. He is on his laptop.*

**MAMA (off-screen):** Sabi! Food don ready!

**SABI:** I dey come!

*Laptop rings: "URGENT — Client".*

**SABI:** On Sunday? This people no get church?

**[CLIP 2]**
*Sabi on the call, camera on, trying to look serious.*

**CLIENT (voice):** Sorry for the weekend call. The site is down. We need you now.

**SABI:** No problem. Let me check.

*Mama enters behind him holding a full plate of jollof rice and chicken.*

**MAMA:** Sabi. Rice.

**SABI:** *(through his teeth)* Mama, I dey meeting.

**MAMA:** Meeting no dey chop? *(sees the screen, waves)* Good afternoon o! Una don chop?

**CLIENT (voice):** Oh! Hello! Is that your... project manager?

**SABI:** ...Yes. Very senior.

**MAMA:** Tell dem say this rice go cold. Cold rice na sin.

**[CLIP 3]**
*Sabi is typing fast. Mama stands behind him, feeding him spoon by spoon like a child. He does not stop typing.*

**CLIENT (voice):** Any update?

**SABI:** *(mouth full)* Mm-hm. Fin' the bug.

*He hits Enter. The screen turns green: "Site restored."*

**CLIENT (voice):** It's back! You're incredible. How do you stay so focused under pressure?

*Wide shot: Mama wiping his mouth with a napkin, Crypto Guy eating from a second plate on the bed, Landlord at the door holding his own spoon.*

**SABI:** I have a very strong support team.

*Call ends. Mama inspects the empty plate, satisfied.*

**MAMA:** You see? Na this rice fix that thing. No be you.

**SABI (narration):** You see, for this compound, nobody understand my work. But everybody dey for the work.

*Mama pauses at the door.*

**MAMA:** That oyinbo people. Dem dey pay overtime for Sunday? Because the rice no be free.

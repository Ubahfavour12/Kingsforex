I get five thousand naira, and I must choose between light and internet. I no fit get the two.
Na Thursday. My salary never enter. For my account, five thousand. For my generator, fuel don finish. For my phone, data remain two hundred MB.
And by eight o'clock tonight, I get meeting with client wey fit give me six months contract.
Simple maths. Fuel na five thousand. Data na five thousand.
If I buy fuel, I go get light, but no internet. I go just siddon for bright room dey look wall.
If I buy data, I go get internet, but my laptop go die for middle of meeting.
I try be smart. I go meet Chairman. I say, "You get inverter. Make I charge." E say im inverter don spoil, but e fit sell me power bank.
I look am. The power bank na empty case. Sand dey inside.
I go meet Iya Bose. "Abeg, borrow me data." She say, "Na sugar? How person dey borrow data?"
I try the neighbour Wi-Fi. Password don change. Dem name the network, "Pay your own."
Time: seven o'clock. One hour remain.
So I buy data. I say I go manage battery. I reduce brightness sotay I no fit see my own screen. I off Bluetooth. I off everything. I dey breathe small small make laptop no hear.
Eight o'clock. Meeting start. Client face show. Battery, fifteen percent.
E dey talk long. "Tell me about yourself." Oga, no time! Ten percent. I dey answer fast fast like auctioneer.
Seven percent. E say, "One last question." Five percent. Three.
You see, as I wan answer, something happen wey I no plan for. Light come. NEPA! For the first time since Monday! I plug charger. I smile like person wey win election.
The client smile too. E say, "You are hired. One small thing. We like your energy, but your video too dark. Next time, abeg, on your light."
I say yes sir. I end the call. I stand up make I on the bulb. Na that time NEPA take am back.

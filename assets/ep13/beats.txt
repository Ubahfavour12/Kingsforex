For my house, nothing on this earth important reach Sunday rice. Not even the client wey dey pay for the rice.
Sunday, two o'clock. Mama don dey cook since morning. Jollof. The one wey the bottom dey burn small, the correct one. The smell don enter my room, don enter my brain.
Na that time my phone ring. Client. Emergency. "Our site is down. We need you now."
I look the phone. I look the kitchen. Mama shout, "Food don ready! If you no come now, I no go call you twice!"
For this house, that statement na law. If you miss the first call, meat don finish. My small brother dey there. That boy dey chop like say dem dey pursue am.
So I carry the laptop come dining table. I say I go do the two. One hand for keyboard, one hand for spoon.
Mama look me. "You bring work come my table?" I say Mama, na emergency. She say, "Na so your papa dey do. See as e be." My papa dey sit down there. E just dey chew.
Client dey video call. I off my camera. I dey type with left hand. Server error everywhere. My brother don carry my meat. I see am. I no fit talk, I dey for call.
Then client say, "Please turn on your camera, we need to see your screen." I on am by mistake. The whole dining table show. Mama, Papa, my brother with two meat for mouth, and one big pot of jollof.
Silence. Client say, "Is that... jollof rice?"
Before I answer, Mama collect the laptop. She face the camera. "Good afternoon. You dey disturb my son for Sunday. You don chop?"
Client say no, e never chop, na since morning e dey work.
You see, na here the meeting change. My mama begin lecture am. Twenty minutes. About rest. About family. About how money no be everything.
The client dey nod. E even dey write am down.
Site come back by itself. E say, "Your mother is very wise. Give her my regards."
Monday, I open my email. Na from the client. "Please, can your mother join our next team meeting?"

My cousin ask me for small favour. The small favour take four months, and e still dey vex for me.
E call me Sunday evening. Sweet voice. "Bros, you know say you be the only tech person for this family." When person start like that, hold your wallet.
E say e wan start business. E dey sell shoe. E need website. "Simple one. Just small thing. Like Amazon." Like Amazon. Small thing.
I say okay, how much you budget? E laugh. "Budget ke? We be family."
I for refuse. But e don already call my mama. And my mama call me. "Your own blood. You go charge am? Na so dem train you?"
So I start. Free. Week one, I show am design. E say the blue no blue enough. I change am. E say now e too blue.
Week three, e say make the logo big. Week four, bigger. By week six, the logo don cover the shoe. E say, "Yes! Now you dey get am."
E dey call me midnight. "Bros, I get idea. Make the website dey play music when person open am." I say no. E report me to my mama again.
Then e send me voice note, four minutes, to explain say the button suppose "feel more expensive."
Month four, website ready. E fine. Honestly, na one of my best work. I tell am say remain just the hosting, twenty thousand per year, make e pay make the site go live.
E quiet. Then e say, "So after everything, you still wan collect money from me?" Twenty thousand! No be even for me, na for the server!
E vex. E tell the whole family say I do am strong thing. For family WhatsApp group, my aunty type, "Money don change am." Uncle add, "Na so dem dey start."
You see, I no fight. I just do one thing. I pay the twenty thousand myself. Site go live. I send the link to the family group.
Everybody open am. Everybody praise am. My cousin begin collect orders that same night.
Next morning e call me. Sweet voice again. "Bros. Customers dey ask for app."

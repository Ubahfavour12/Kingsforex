I dey earn dollar from my bedroom, but my mama dey pray make God give me job.
Every morning, six o'clock, she go knock my door. "You still dey sleep? Your mates don reach office!"
Mama, my office na this bed. My oga dey California. E never even sleep.
But try explain am. I tell her say I be software developer. She ask me, "So na you dey repair phone?" I say no. "Computer?" No. "Then wetin you dey develop?" I say website.
She look me well well, come say, "So you no get work."
E come worse the day wey her friend, Mama Chinedu, come visit. Mama Chinedu pikin na banker. Suit, tie, ID card for neck. Every sentence, "My son for bank, my son for bank."
Then she turn face me. "And you, wetin you dey do?" Before I open mouth, my mama don answer for me. "E dey press laptop."
Press laptop! Like say I be DJ.
So I decide say I go prove am. I carry my laptop come parlour. I show them the app wey I build. People dey use am for three countries.
Mama Chinedu look the screen, she nod. She say, "E fine. But where your ID card?"
I no get ID card. I get Slack.
That night I vex. I transfer fifty thousand naira give my mama. No explanation. Just alert.
You see, na here the matter turn. Because that alert do wetin four years of explanation no fit do.
Next morning, six o'clock, nobody knock my door. Seven o'clock, nothing. I come out by ten, I see food for table. Rice. Two meat.
Mama dey whisper for phone: "No disturb am, e dey work." Compound people come dey greet me, "Engineer! Engineer!"
I think say I don win. Until Sunday, for church, pastor say make anybody wey God bless that week come out for testimony.
My mama stand up. She collect microphone. She say, "I thank God for my son. E still no get work. But the laptop don dey pay."

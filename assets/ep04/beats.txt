My house rent increase by one hundred thousand naira because my landlord see wetin dey my hand.
For three years, I dey hide am. Whenever Baba Landlord dey come, I go close laptop, carry exercise book, dey look like student.
Because for this compound, Baba Landlord no dey charge rent by room. E dey charge by face.
If you buy new curtain, rent go up. You paint your door, rent go up. One woman buy standing fan, e tell her say, "Madam, you don dey comfortable."
So that day, I dey video call with my client. Headphone for ear. I no hear the gate. I no hear the slippers.
I just see shadow for my window.
Baba Landlord stand there. E dey look my screen. Oyinbo man dey talk for inside. E look the oyinbo. E look me. E look the oyinbo again.
E no talk anything. E just smile, waka go. That smile cost me money.
Evening, I see letter under my door. "Dear tenant. Due to your international connections, your rent don review."
International connections! Baba, na Zoom!
I go meet am. I explain. I say the man na my client, e dey pay small small.
Baba Landlord say, "If white man dey inside your room, you be expatriate. Expatriate rent different."
I vex. I say fine. If na so, I go pack. I begin find new house.
But you see, Baba Landlord get one problem wey e no tell anybody. E get twelve tenants and e no sabi who don pay. Everything dey inside one old notebook, and rain don beat the notebook.
So one evening e knock my door. Quiet. No shouting. E say, "This your laptop. E fit remember who dey owe me?"
I build am small app. One evening work. Every tenant, every payment, every date.
E happy sotay e dance. E say, "From today, your rent don reduce!"
I smile. I open the app make I confirm. Na there I see the new line e don add by himself:
"Laptop boy. App maintenance fee: one hundred thousand."

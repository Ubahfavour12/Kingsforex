I sleep as rich man, I wake up as poor man, and I no spend one kobo for night.
Na so the matter be. My client pay me for dollar. Friday night, I check the rate. E sweet.
I calculate am, I see say this month, I go pay rent, buy generator, still chop shawarma.
I say make I change the money tomorrow morning. Wetin fit happen for one night? For this country? Everything.
Saturday, I wake up, I open phone. The rate don fall. No be small fall. Na the kind fall wey person dey hold chest.
My generator don become rechargeable lamp. My shawarma don become gala.
I call the aboki wey dey change money. I say, "Abeg, wetin happen?" E say, "Oga, na market."
I say which market open for midnight? E say, "Dollar no dey sleep."
So I decide say I go wait. E go rise back. I siddon dey refresh. Every five minutes. Refresh. E fall again. Refresh. E fall again.
My mama pass, she see my face. She say, "Who die?" I say, "Dollar." She say, "E be your family?"
By afternoon, Chairman don hear. E come give me advice. "Hold am. No sell. Rate go climb by Monday. Trust me."
That one wey Chairman talk, I know say I must sell immediately.
But I no sell. I stubborn. Sunday, e fall again. I no fit chop. Rice dey my front, I dey see exchange rate inside the stew.
Monday morning, I give up. I change everything. Small money, but I collect am. I say at least the matter don end.
You see, the thing about this rate be say, e dey watch you. Personally.
Ten minutes after I change am, my phone do "ping." Rate don rise. Pass Friday own. Pass any rate wey I don ever see for my life.
Chairman run enter my room dey jump. "I tell you! I tell you make you hold!"
For the first time for im whole life, Chairman correct. And na my money e take correct.
